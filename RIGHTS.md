The numerical CSV files used to produce Supplementary Figure S12 contain summary statistics from the present analysis rather than original source workbooks.

The archive of public numerical research outputs must be reviewed file by file before upload. It must not include private credentials, publisher-provided source workbooks, or material whose distribution terms have not been verified.
