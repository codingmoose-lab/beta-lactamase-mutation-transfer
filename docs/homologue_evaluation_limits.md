# NDM-1/VIM-2 mutation-effect evaluation limits

Cross-protein comparisons use 81 aligned positions with identical wild-type amino acids in both proteins (1,513 measured equivalent substitutions). The 136 aligned positions whose wild-type residues differ are excluded from that direct comparison. Assay concentrations are 256 µg/mL ampicillin for NDM-1 and 128 µg/mL for VIM-2 at 37 °C, as used by the original experiment; the assays are not identical.

Scores at or below the analysis threshold of −4 are represented by −4 in the fitted response. The 478 substitutions above −4 in both proteins comprise a subset selected using measured outcomes and are reported descriptively rather than as a prospective prediction population.

The direct measured-source substitution reference assumes that the equivalent source effect is available. Position-withheld reference models and ESM-C Ridge models exclude source-protein measurements at each held-out homologous position, and use source-protein measurements alone for fitting and parameter selection.

Residue geometry, atomic-local structure and biological reference data describe wild-type structures; the calculations do not provide experimentally measured mutant structures or establish causes of prediction-error differences.
