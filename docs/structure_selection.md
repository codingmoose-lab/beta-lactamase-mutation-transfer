# Experimental structure selection

Candidate chains were compared using assay-sequence correspondence, measured-position coverage, unresolved residues, coordinate inspection, validation reports, and metal-site information.

The principal structures retained for the study are:

- NDM-1: PDB 3SPU, chain B;
- VIM-2: PDB 5YD7, chain B.

VIM-2 chain A is retained as a secondary chain for sensitivity checks. The additional zinc site observed in 5YD7 is recorded separately from the two selected catalytic zinc sites and is not used as a catalytic feature.

These choices were retained after the sequence, coverage, coordinate, active-site, metal-site, and validation checks represented in `data/interim/protein_structures/`. Experimental PDB structures remain the primary structural source for structural feature generation.
